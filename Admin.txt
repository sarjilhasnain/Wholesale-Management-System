USER STORIES - ADMIN
=====================

US1 | Log in to the system
Benefit: Secure access to system features
Acceptance: I enter valid username and password and successfully access the dashboard.
Feature Area: Authentication

US2 | Add new shoe products
Benefit: Products are available for sale
Acceptance: I enter product name, brand, size, category, price, and stock quantity. Product appears in product list.
Feature Area: Product Management

US3 | Update product details
Benefit: Product information stays accurate
Acceptance: I edit product details and save changes. Updated information is visible immediately.
Feature Area: Product Management

US5 | Add customer information
Benefit: Easy customer tracking
Acceptance: I enter customer details and save. Customer profile is created successfully.
Feature Area: Customer Management

US7 | Generate invoice
Benefit: Accurate billing
Acceptance: Invoice is automatically generated with correct totals and stored in the system.
Feature Area: Sales & Billing

US10 | Manage supplier information
Benefit: Smooth product supply
Acceptance: I add or update supplier details and save successfully.
Feature Area: Supplier Management

US15 | Manage user roles and permissions
Benefit: Secure system access
Acceptance: I assign roles (Retailer, Shop Manager, Delivery Man) and define permissions successfully.
Feature Area: Authentication / Admin
